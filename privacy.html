Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

We never keep your sides or takes, and nothing is ever used to train AI.

The short version

Your sides, scripts and takes stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, and nothing you make in the app is sent to us.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your auditions, scripts and sides (PDFs): stored inside the app on your iPhone.
  • Your takes and slates: stored inside the app on your iPhone. If you choose, the app saves copies to your Photos library.
  • Recorded reader lines: a voice memo or video you bring in (from a friend or yourself) is stored inside the app and split into lines on your iPhone.
  • Reader voices: the automated reader uses your iPhone's own built-in voices. Its audio is made on your iPhone.
  • Your name and profile choices: stored on your iPhone, used for take file names and slates.
  • A small app log: to help fix bugs, the app keeps a short technical log on your iPhone of what it did and when (the last 14 days, at most 2 MB). It never includes your sides, takes or the words it heard, and the app never sends it. If you report a problem, we may ask you to send it, and that's your choice.

Your iPhone's own iCloud Backup may include the app's data, under Apple's terms and your backup settings. We never see it.

What leaves your iPhone, and why

  • Reader on another device: the reader's audio goes directly between your own devices over your local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. It doesn't go through us.
  • Subscriptions: Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. The app checks with Apple whether your subscription is active.
  • Usage statistics: see What we collect below. You can turn them off.

What the app uses, and why

  • Camera and microphone: to record your self-tapes, slates and, if you choose, the reader's lines yourself; and to scan paper sides.
  • Speech recognition: to follow along as you and your reader speak, and to find each line in a recorded reader. It runs on your iPhone using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is not sent to us.
  • Local network: only to find and connect to your own device when you play the reader from it.
  • Photos: to save takes to your library when you ask, and to pick a video for a recorded reader.
  • Notifications: for due-date reminders, created on your phone.
  • Files and the share sheet: to bring in sides, voice memos and videos you choose, and to send takes you choose.

What we collect

The app has no advertising and doesn't track you across other apps. To learn what to make better, it sends us usage statistics: which screens and buttons are used, how long takes are, whether the script kept up, and crash and speed reports. They never include your sides, scripts, takes or voice. They're tied to a random ID for your install, not to your name. PostHog (EU) processes them for us. You can turn them off any time in Profile > Settings > Share usage to improve Self Tape.

If you turn on "Help improve script following", the app also sends the lines where the script lost you and the words it heard at those moments, and nothing else from your sides.

Nothing you make in the app, including your acting and your scripts, is ever used to train AI.

We keep usage statistics for up to 12 months, then delete them. After that we keep only totals and trends.

Test versions

If you test Self Tape through TestFlight, the test version also sends us a test log: how you use the app and the words it heard, never your videos, audio or sides. Test logs are deleted 90 days after they're sent.

Launch email list

If you sign up on walnuttechnologiesllc.com to hear when Self Tape is out, we keep your email address in a private Google Form owned by Walnut Technologies LLC. We use it only to send you that announcement, never share it, and delete the list after launch. To be removed sooner, email hello@walnuttechnologiesllc.com.

Sharing

We don't sell or share data. When you send a take, or send your sides to a reader, it goes where you send it (for example Mail, Messages, AirDrop or a casting site), under that service's terms.

Children

Self Tape isn't directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete an audition, take or recorded reader in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Copies you saved to Photos or shared elsewhere stay where you put them.

Your sides and takes never reach us. Usage statistics are tied only to a random install ID. To have yours deleted, email us from the app's Report a problem screen and include the ID shown there.

Changes

If a future version adds anything like accounts, cloud storage or usage statistics, we'll update this policy first and, where required, ask for your consent.
